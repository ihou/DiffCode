**macOS:** [Download DiffCode.pkg](https://github.com/ihou/DiffCode/releases/download/v0.4.6/DiffCode.pkg) for Apple Silicon Macs, or [DiffCode-Intel.pkg](https://github.com/ihou/DiffCode/releases/download/v0.4.6/DiffCode-Intel.pkg) for Intel Macs — signed and notarized by Apple; copies already installed update themselves. Prefer to drag it in? Use `DiffCode.zip` or `DiffCode-Intel.zip`. Requires macOS 14 or later. Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" is Apple Silicon, "Processor: … Intel …" is Intel.

**Windows:** [Download DiffCode-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/ihou/DiffCode/releases/download/v0.4.6/DiffCode-Setup-x64.exe) — installs for your account, no administrator rights needed; or unzip `DiffCode-windows-x64.zip` and run it. Requires 64-bit Windows 10 (version 1809) or later. The installer is not code-signed, so on first run Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info → Run anyway**.

### New

- **Whole commit messages in the Log ([#8](https://github.com/ihou/DiffCode/issues/8)).** The details under the Log showed at most four lines of a commit's message, often little more than its title. They now show all of it. A long message scrolls in place, and the commit's files keep their room below. This is for both the Mac and the Windows app.

### Fixes

- **Picking a commit in the Log (Windows).** Picking another commit, by click or with the arrow keys, could leave the previous commit's files, message and diff on screen. They now follow what you pick, and so does the diff when you pick another file of the commit.

### 新功能

- **在提交历史里查看完整的提交说明（[#8](https://github.com/ihou/DiffCode/issues/8)）。**以前提交历史下方的详情最多只显示 4 行提交说明，常常只能看到标题。现在显示全文：说明很长时可以在原处滚动，下面的文件列表仍然保留位置。Mac 版和 Windows 版都有。

### 修复

- **在提交历史里切换提交（Windows 版）。**用鼠标点击或方向键切换到另一个提交后，文件列表、提交说明和 diff 可能还停留在上一个提交。现在会跟着切换；在提交里换一个文件时，diff 也会跟着换。

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt` (macOS) and `SHA256SUMS-windows.txt` (Windows).
