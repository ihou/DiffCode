DiffCode 0.4.6

新功能

  • 在提交历史里查看完整的提交说明。以前提交历史下方的详情最多只显示 4 行提交说明，常常只能看到标题。现在显示全文：说明很长时可以在原处滚动，下面的文件列表仍然保留位置。

New

  • Whole commit messages in the Log. The details under the Log showed at most four lines of a commit's message, often little more than its title. They now show all of it. A long message scrolls in place, and the commit's files keep their room below.
